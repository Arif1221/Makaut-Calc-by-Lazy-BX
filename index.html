Rules & reference

Built around the method printed on MAKAUT grade cards. Not affiliated with the university.

Regular · 4-year B.Tech

  • Eight semesters: Sem 1–8.
  • YGPA is the credit-weighted mean of each academic year.
  • Running CGPA is cumulative credit index ÷ cumulative credits.

DGPA = (Y1 + Y2 + 1.5 Y3 + 1.5 Y4) / 5

Lateral · 3-year B.Tech

  • Six B.Tech semesters: Sem 3–8.
  • Entry is at 2nd year; calculation starts with Sem 3.
  • Diploma performance is not mixed into B.Tech CGPA.

DGPA = (Y2 + 1.5 Y3 + 1.5 Y4) / 4

FAQ

Does the calculator save my results?

Yes. Semester entries, programme selection, known credits and subject rows stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Why isn’t CGPA the simple average of SGPAs?

MAKAUT’s cumulative calculation is credit-weighted. A 24-credit semester contributes more than an 18-credit semester.

What happens if I enter 95% in Percentage → GPA?

It is rejected. Under the stated formula, GPA 10 corresponds to 92.50%, so a higher percentage cannot map onto the 10-point scale.

Why does final DGPA stay blank?

Every required semester must be complete. Regular programmes need Sem 1–8; lateral entry needs Sem 3–8.

Is this an official MAKAUT calculator?

No. It is an independent student utility. For formal applications, follow the receiving organisation and use official university records.
